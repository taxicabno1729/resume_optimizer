Senior Full Stack Developer

About Us:
TechInnovate Inc. is a fast-growing technology company specializing in cloud-based enterprise solutions. We are looking for a talented Senior Full Stack Developer to join our engineering team and help us build the next generation of our flagship product.

Job Description:
We are seeking an experienced Full Stack Developer with expertise in modern web technologies to design, develop, and maintain our cloud-based applications. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in JavaScript frameworks, particularly React and Node.js, and experience with cloud infrastructure.

Responsibilities:
• Design and develop responsive, scalable web applications using React.js and Node.js
• Build and maintain RESTful APIs and microservices
• Optimize application performance and ensure high availability
• Implement security best practices and data protection measures
• Collaborate with UX/UI designers to implement intuitive user interfaces
• Participate in code reviews and provide constructive feedback
• Mentor junior developers and promote best practices
• Contribute to the continuous improvement of our development processes
• Work in an Agile environment with daily standups and sprint planning

Requirements:
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field
• 5+ years of experience in full-stack development
• Strong proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3
• Extensive experience with React.js, Redux, and related ecosystem
• Proficiency with Node.js and Express.js for backend development
• Experience with RESTful API design and implementation
• Knowledge of at least one SQL database (PostgreSQL preferred) and NoSQL databases (MongoDB)
• Familiarity with DevOps tools such as Docker, Jenkins, and AWS
• Experience with version control systems (Git)
• Strong understanding of web security principles
• Experience with test-driven development and testing frameworks
• Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automated testing

Preferred Qualifications:
• Experience with TypeScript and GraphQL
• Knowledge of cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
• Experience with containerization and orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes)
• Familiarity with serverless architecture
• Experience with WebSocket for real-time applications
• Understanding of accessibility standards and compliance
• Contributions to open-source projects
